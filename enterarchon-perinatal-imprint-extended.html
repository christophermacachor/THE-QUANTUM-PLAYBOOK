1. Introduction: Evolutionary Mismatch and the Missing Data

The human spine evolved over millions of years for a dynamic, bipedal lifestyle characterized by upright posture, varied movement, and intermittent physical activity (Taçyıldız et al., 2026). The agricultural and industrial revolutions introduced static, flexed, and ergonomically suboptimal postures—prolonged sitting, repetitive bending, and heavy lifting—that deviate from the spine's evolutionary design parameters. This evolutionary mismatch underlies the rising prevalence of spinal disorders and low back pain.

The Enterarchon framework extends this evolutionary mismatch analysis to the birth canal. Bipedalism is not a "natural" evolutionary progression but a forced adaptation to changing environmental conditions (forests to savannas). The human pelvis—shaped by bipedalism—creates the birth canal geometry that imprints the scalar tunnel.

  2. Water Birth Prediction: The framework predicts that water birth—which minimizes both compressive gravitational forces and harsh environmental exposure—produces NDEs with minimal spatial structure (formless voids or instantaneous non-local awareness).

This unified analysis integrates the evolutionary mismatch literature (Taçyıldız et al., 2026), the aqua-terrestrial design hypothesis, and the growing body of waterbirth research to validate the Enterarchon framework.
