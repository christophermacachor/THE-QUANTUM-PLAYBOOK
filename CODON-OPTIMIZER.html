Prime Editing & Gene Therapy Context

Reference: Liu Z, Guo D, Wang D, Zhou J, Chen Q, Lai J. Prime editing: A gene precision editing tool from inception to present. The FASEB Journal. 2024;38:e70148. DOI: 10.1096/fj.202401692R

This review covers the development of prime editing (PE) as a precise gene editing tool that avoids double-strand breaks. It details PE mechanisms, improvements (PE2–PE6, pegRNA optimization, split-AAV delivery), and applications in sickle cell disease, beta-thalassemia, PKU, DMD, Huntington's, inherited retinal diseases, and RDEB. PE can correct up to 97.5% of COL7A1 mutations in RDEB and achieve therapeutic corrections in mouse models.

Relevance to codon optimization: For gene therapy, codon optimization of therapeutic genes (e.g., HBB, PAH, DMD) is critical for efficient expression in target cells. The optimizer above designs expression-optimized sequences for human, mouse, or other hosts, complementing the precision of prime editing.

Additional resources: Plasmids, protocols, and tools for prime editing are available at gitlab.com/sanjanalab/primeediting (clone: git@gitlab.com:sanjanalab/primeediting.git).
