🔮 What is a Scalar Field?

Imagine a giant, invisible blanket that covers the entire universe. Every point on this blanket has a value — like temperature or height.

A scalar field is like that blanket. It's a field of numbers that fills all of space. When you move, the numbers change — and that change creates energy, motion, and even gravity!

✨ Think of it like: ripples in a pond that never stop moving.
